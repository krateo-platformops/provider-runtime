QUESTION. Is the long drain's target S* determined? The engine's fixed-capacity curve of the drain plant gives S* of
3.9, 4.8 and 4.3 on seeds 9001-9003, non-monotone, with U nearly flat in S near the target (the review of c1acaa4). This
measures the real curve on real cgroups, to see whether it is as flat and as spread.

DECISION RULE, pre-registered (the reviewer's, verbatim): "If the 95% CI of S* is narrower than the band's half-width
above (S*·3r̄ ≈ 15%), the band is judged with the measured S*; otherwise determinacy fails and the long drain's band is
reported, not judged." Narrower means the CI's full width (upper minus lower) is under 0.15·Ŝ*, Ŝ* the point
estimate: a resolution of about ±0.3 slots at S* ≈ 4. Both outcomes are acceptable in advance: a determined S*
replaces the engine's frozen value in the gate's long-drain band; an undetermined one leaves the band reported.
"Determined" means the bootstrap's 2.5-97.5% interval is finite and narrower than 0.15·Ŝ*: a resample whose curve
doesn't cross ρ·L sorts to the end it lies at, -inf if every mean is above it and +inf if every mean is below.

POWER, against the budget, stated plainly. The slope of U at the crossing is about 0.01-0.02 cores per slot (the
reviewer), and the spread of a 30-minute run's mean U about 0.011 cores (the reviewer's estimate, from the engine).
S* is interpolated between the two bracketing n, so its standard error is about 0.71·SE(mean U at a bracketing n)
divided by the slope (equal weights, the slope's own error left out, so this is optimistic). With k runs at each
bracketing n, SE(mean U) = 0.011/√k, and the CI's full width about 2·1.96·SE(S*):
  k = 13:         SE(mean U) 0.0031; width about 0.43 slots at slope 0.02 (under 0.6: determined), 0.85 at 0.01 (not).
  k = 4 (stage 1): SE(mean U) 0.0055; width about 0.76 slots at slope 0.02 (not determined), 1.53 at 0.01 (not).
  To be determined at slope 0.01 the width must be under about 0.6 slots, which needs k of about 26 at each
  bracketing n: about 21 h for the three bracketing n alone. The plan can't afford that. The reviewer's k of about
  4-13 is the k that brings SE(mean U) to 0.003-0.006, a ±1 SE resolution of about ±0.3 slots, not a 95% CI. So:
  a budget of k = 13 determines S* only if the slope is near 0.02; k = 4 will most likely end with "determinacy
  fails", which the rule accepts in advance. Hence the two stages: stage 1 measures the slope and the spread, and
  stage 2 runs only if they say k* ≤ 13 suffices. The bootstrap reports the width actually reached.

WORKLOAD, and how faithful it is to the engine's drain plant (sim, drainHeavy: 3000 ms of CPU, then 28500 ms of wait):
  mode curve (prun.sh): 250m CPU limit (request = limit), 512Mi, 64 Greetings, poll 1s, MaxConcurrentReconciles = n.
  NO_GOVERNOR=true: no rate-limit governor is set up or passed to the reconciler, so the n workers alone bound the
  reconciles in flight (main.go). Each Observe spends a drawn CPU time measured on its own locked OS thread (burn.go's
  thread CPU clock), then sleeps a drawn wait: CPU first, as the engine. Both are drawn as the engine draws them,
  max(0.1·x, x·(1 + 0.1·N(0,1))), from LOAD_CPU_MS=3000 and LOAD_HOLD_MS=28500 with LOAD_JITTER=0.1, seeded per run
  (LOAD_SEED, the cksum of the run's name): without it the plant is periodic and phase-locked, and its between-run
  spread could be an artefact (the reviewer). GOMAXPROCS=2, set explicitly, as the engine's Procs 2 (two threads
  running Go code at once; each burn holds one).
  Not the same as the engine: the controller's own work per reconcile (a Get, the status update) adds a few ms; the
  process has a background (informers, GC, none of which the engine has); the node's other CPUs run GKE's system pods.
  No liveness probe in this mode: the curve is the plant's, and a probe killed by throttling would cut runs short.
  The image: build.sh main <tag> (the harness against this worktree, main's option names, opts_main.go), pushed to
  the registry set up with the cluster; its digest is recorded in results/image-<tag>.txt.

POINTS AND ORDER. n in {3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8}: runs concentrated at the n bracketing the expected crossing (4, 5, 6), fewer at
  the anchors 3, 7, 8. Within each repetition the order is randomized (Python random.Random(20261110), shuffled per
  repetition), and repetitions are interleaved, never blocked by n:
  stage 1: rep 1: 4 3 7 5 6; rep 2: 8 4 5 6; rep 3: 3 5 4 6 7; rep 4: 5 4 6 8
  stage 2: the bracketing pair, re-randomized per repetition with random.Random(20261111) (curve_block.sh stage2).
  Runs are named pc-n<n>-r<rep> in stage 1 and pc-n<n>-s2-r<rep> in stage 2; a re-run adds -a<attempt>.

LENGTH. 2100 s per run: the first 300 s discarded as warm-up (at least 3 lifetimes at the cap: the drain plant's
  life at the cap is about 81 s at n = 7, from its frozen curve, and shorter at fewer slots), then 1800 s measured.

THE UNIT IS THE RUN. Per n: the mean U (cgroup usage per second, cores) of each run over its measured 1800 s; the
  per-n mean of those run means, with a t interval on k − 1 degrees of freedom, and the between-run standard
  deviation. Never per-sample intervals: samples are autocorrelated over about 45-80 s. S* is where the per-n means
  cross ρ·L = 0.9 × 0.25 = 0.225 cores, interpolated linearly between the two bracketing n (the first crossing; any
  other crossings are reported, and a non-monotone curve is reported as such). Its CI: a bootstrap over runs (10,000
  resamples, runs resampled within each n, the curve re-interpolated each time; the percentile interval), with the
  delta method on the bracketing means as a check. Resamples with no crossing are counted, reported and sorted to
  ±inf as above.

VALIDITY, checked per run and reported, never used to drop runs selectively:
  - the CPU measured on each burn's own locked thread against what it asked for, over the measured window
    (STAT's burn_cpu_total_ms against burn_req_total_ms), within 1%. The reviewer's form was "the per-call CPU ...
    within 3000 ms ±1%"; with the jitter each call asks for a drawn amount, so the check is the measured against the
    requested, and the mean request (about 3000 ms) is reported. The cgroup's CPU per completion is reported beside
    it with its edge allowance, ±n/C (the reconciles in flight at the window's two ends), not judged;
  - completions counted;
  - throttled periods per sample (Δnr_throttled/Δnr_periods per second) reported;
  - the node: a dedicated single-node pool; its name and CPU model are identical across all runs, and a change stops
    the block (results/curve-node.txt holds the first run's);
  - the PSI stall distribution recorded: avg10 every second, and the per-second share from "some total".
  A run that fails a check is re-run at the same n and position, and both the failure and the re-run are reported.
